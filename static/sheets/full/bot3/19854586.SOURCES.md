# Options open interest as a % of market cap

Library PNG for My Bot's Sheets tab "Options OI % of Market Cap" (bot3 gid `19854586`). The image is Steve's monthly Python render (black / X-dark background, grey bars, MSTR in orange). The Google Sheet tab is real, so Open in Sheets stays on. Monday's importer will not overwrite this PNG (`KEEP_MANUAL_PNGS`).

Current image: close of 25 November 2026 (retrieved 26 November 2026). MSTR is highlighted.

## Metric (Strategy.com definition)

Open interest notional is total outstanding call and put contracts, across every listed expiration and strike, times 100 shares, times the latest share price. The chart plots that notional divided by market cap. MSTR is always included. The other names are re-screened each month: the top 12 US-listed stocks by this ratio, market cap at least $2B, at least 50,000 option contracts of open interest, at least five listed expirations, and at least 5,000 contracts traded on the as-of session. ETFs, funds, notes and preferreds are excluded.

## Sources

- Open interest and prices: Nasdaq.com option chains (all expirations), cross-checked against Yahoo Finance option chains.
- Market caps: Yahoo Finance quote `marketCap`, cross-checked against the Nasdaq screener and SEC cover-page shares outstanding times price. MSTR uses Strategy.com's own market cap (class A and B).
- Universe: Nasdaq stock screener (NYSE, Nasdaq, and NYSE American listings).
- Definition and MSTR reference figures: [strategy.com/options](https://www.strategy.com/options) and [strategy.com/notes](https://www.strategy.com/notes).
